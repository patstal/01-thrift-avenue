About Us

We are students at King's College London in the Digital Humanities department. This project is done anonymously by a single student who has always paid very close attention to what is going on around the world. Social and environmental fairness have been pressing issues for them, fashion and design are the biggest interests. So why not combine everything together and find a solution that tackles these issues and is fun at the same time? This question brought 01 Thrift Avenue to life. The creator realised quickly that many great ideas have still not been implemented. 01TA is based on the simple idea of social networks and platforms, without financial greed. We enable shop owners of disadvantaged societal groups to get a voice online who find it difficult to run an online presence individually. Also, in recent years second hand stores have been vastly commercialised by big vintage shop chains all across London and the world. We aim to take over market dominance and thereby give it back to independent shop owners. 65 percent of the profit generated goes to them, so the more people use our platform the better off they will be. Bring it on!
